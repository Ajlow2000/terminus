alec lowry — logo concepts

Four combination-mark directions, each in full-color and monochrome. Wordmark uses Newsreader to match the site. Mono variants use currentColor, so they invert cleanly on any background.

1 — Summit

Outdoor-forward

Layered mountain peaks with a notched ridge. The most literal nod to the outdoor side of the brand. Reads well at small sizes and makes a strong app-icon if you crop to the icon only.

3 — Contour

Balanced

Topographic contour rings around a summit marker. The most balanced of the four: topo maps read as both outdoor (real maps) and technical (precision instrumentation, isolines). Quietly distinctive — fewer brands occupy this territory.

4 — Cairn

Outdoor-forward, conceptual

A stack of trail-marker stones. Cairns mean "you're on the path" to anyone who hikes — a memorable image that maps neatly onto a builder's craft (stacking the right pieces). Slight stone tilts keep it organic, not corporate.
